Helsinki felt very clean to me, but there were many tourists compared to the three Baltic states.

I didn't have many plans since I was only there briefly. Just wandered around the capital.

I visited the Old Market Hall and wandered around the market area, looking at various foods and souvenirs—many related to reindeer and totems.

I also visited several famous churches, like Helsinki Cathedral, Uspenski Cathedral, and the Rock Church that's built directly into the rock.

I also went to find my brother's favorite Moomins store and brought back a small night light.

On this trip, I also tried reindeer meat and elk meat for the first time. One of them had a slightly stronger gamey taste, but it was still acceptable—quite a unique experience.
