Beijing was my first city in China, and somehow, a lot of the moments still feel very clear.

The most unforgettable one was watching the sunrise at Summer Palace. It was incredibly beautiful—but also incredibly cold. The kind of cold that makes you question your life choices while taking photos. Still, thinking back now, it was absolutely worth it.

Forbidden City felt completely different. Endless buildings, red walls, and rooftops stretching far beyond what you can see. No matter how much you walk, it always feels like you’ve only seen a small part of it. I didn’t try to see everything that day—I just kept walking, looking, and letting the place speak for itself.

Beijing feels vast, but also unhurried. Some scenes are overwhelming, while others are quiet enough that all you notice is the sound of the wind and your footsteps. As my first city in China, it didn’t leave me with shock—it left me with something slow and lasting.
