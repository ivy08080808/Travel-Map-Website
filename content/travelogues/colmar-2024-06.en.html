My reason for coming to Colmar was quite simple.

After watching "Howl's Moving Castle," I had always been drawn to those fairytale-like towns, thinking that if such places really existed in reality, they would be very gentle.

When I actually walked into Colmar, I found it truly beautiful. The town has many, many storks standing on rooftops and by chimneys, and they don't look out of place at all—instead, they seem like scenery that should naturally be here. The streets are also filled with raisin bread everywhere, and when I saw it, I suddenly thought of something I learned in physics class, finding it both funny and magical.

That day, I didn't really do anything special—just walked around the town, slowly spending the day. No rushing, no must-see destinations, just simply being there. As I walked, I kept thinking that if I had the chance, I'd like to come back in winter to see what this town would look like in another season.
