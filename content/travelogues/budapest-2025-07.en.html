Budapest is a city with incredibly beautiful architecture.

The first place I found especially fascinating was the Hungarian Parliament Building. The building itself is truly extravagant—stunning in daylight, and even more dramatic once it lights up at night. Standing by the Danube, I couldn’t stop taking photos; every angle looks like a postcard.

Next, I went to Buda Castle. Sitting up on the hill, the view is wide and calm, and the whole city feels quiet and expansive from above. But the climb up there wasn’t easy at all. In the moment my legs were just sore, but looking back now, that view was absolutely worth it.

I also really loved Fisherman’s Bastion. The white towers and arches feel almost fairytale-like, and when you stand there looking out over the city, you briefly forget you’ve been walking all day.

Overall, Budapest is truly beautiful—but it’s also truly hot in summer.
