It was cloudy when we went to the beach that day, but the sea was still a vast stretch of blue tinged with green, meeting a pebble shore. People sat by the water, lay down, stared into the distance—like no one had anywhere they absolutely needed to be. We picnicked on the rocks, eating cheap baguette from the supermarket, and it made me feel like happiness doesn’t actually have to cost much.

Those days, we didn’t do anything in particular. We just walked along the coast, watched the waves roll in, then slip back out again. Time feels softer here; wherever you go, you don’t really want to check your watch.

Nice isn’t the kind of city that makes you gasp in amazement, but it’s perfect for the moments when you want to think about nothing at all.
