Tallinn is a city perfect for slow walks.

The streets are quiet, the air is cold, but clear and refreshing.

I visited Fat Margaret, where the thick city walls give a strong sense of security and directly remind you that this is a very old city.

Then I visited many churches, and the one that left the deepest impression was Alexander Nevsky Cathedral. The domes, onion-shaped roofs, and Orthodox style are very distinctive. Just standing outside, it has a strong presence, and inside it's so quiet that you unconsciously slow your steps.

Later, I walked all the way up to Toompea Hill.

Standing high and looking down, you can see the entire city's rooftops and the distant sea. In that moment, you suddenly understand why this city feels so steady, so slow.
